# Chapel on the Go™

Source for **[chapelonthego.org](https://chapelonthego.org)**.

Chapel on the Go provides ready-to-share devotionals, prayers and Bible studies for NFL agencies, NFL and NCAA teams, team chaplains, and marketplace leaders and the teams they lead, along with culture talks for teams in crisis.

Chapel on the Go is a ministry of **Champions Unleashed**.

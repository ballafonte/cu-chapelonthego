What's included

Chapel, ready to share.

Every piece is anchored in Scripture and written in the language of the game.

Weekly Devotionals

Swipeable, phone-first devotionals players can read in the locker room, on the bus or in the hotel the night before a game.

Prayers for Every Season

Prayers for game week, injury and recovery, cut week, mental health and the moments when the season gets heavy.

Facility Bible Studies

Bible studies led alongside athletes at team facilities, with discussion questions that meet players where they are.

Shareable Formats

Content formatted for chaplains, coaches, agents and leaders to send directly to their people, so the message reaches them wherever they are.

Culture Talks for Teams in Crisis

When a team is going through a losing streak, a leadership change, a layoff or a loss, we talk with the team about culture, trust and unity, and help it find the way forward. For locker rooms and workplaces alike.
